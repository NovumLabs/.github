# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Novum Labs is committed to creating an open, welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone who participates in our projects and community.

We welcome contributions from people of all backgrounds, experience levels, nationalities, and professional disciplines. We value curiosity, scientific integrity, thoughtful discussion, and collaboration.

We pledge to make participation in the Novum Labs community a respectful experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, personal appearance, background, education, experience level, or other personal characteristics.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Treating everyone with respect, patience, and professionalism.
- Providing constructive feedback and accepting it gracefully.
- Welcoming different perspectives, ideas, and approaches.
- Focusing criticism on technical ideas, research methods, and code rather than individuals.
- Supporting newcomers and helping others learn.
- Giving appropriate credit to original authors, researchers, and contributors.
- Reporting errors, limitations, and research results honestly.
- Respecting intellectual property, software licenses, data rights, and privacy.
- Communicating clearly and collaborating in good faith.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Threats, abusive language, or deliberately humiliating others.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or repeated bad-faith arguments.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Plagiarism, misrepresentation of contributions, or knowingly falsifying research results.
- Sharing confidential information, credentials, or sensitive data without authorization.
- Deliberately disrupting project infrastructure or misusing community resources.
- Any other conduct that is inappropriate for a respectful and professional community.

## Scientific and Engineering Integrity

Novum Labs encourages open inquiry, experimentation, and respectful disagreement.

Community members should:

- Distinguish experimental findings from established results.
- Document relevant assumptions, methods, limitations, and sources.
- Avoid fabricating benchmarks, test results, citations, or model capabilities.
- Respect dataset licenses and the rights of content creators.
- Identify meaningful contributions and acknowledge collaborators.
- Discuss technical disagreements using evidence and reasoned arguments.

Good-faith technical criticism is welcome. Disagreement about a method, result, or design decision is not, by itself, a violation of this Code.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to participation in Novum Labs projects and community spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories, issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Project-related communication channels and events.
- Public spaces where an individual is representing a Novum Labs project or community.

Project maintainers may establish additional guidelines where appropriate, provided they are consistent with this Code.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for addressing reported or observed violations within the scope of their authority.

Depending on the circumstances, responses may include:

1. A private or public reminder of the expected standards.
2. A warning and a request to correct the behavior.
3. Temporary restrictions on participation in project spaces.
4. Removal of content or contributions that violate community standards.
5. Suspension or permanent exclusion from a project or community space for serious or repeated violations.

Actions will be proportionate to the circumstances, with consideration for the severity, impact, and history of the behavior.

Maintainers should handle reports as discreetly as reasonably possible and avoid unnecessary disclosure of personal information.

## Reporting a Concern

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code, contact the maintainers privately through an established contact channel listed in the relevant repository or on the official Novum Labs website.

Please include, where possible:

- A description of the incident.
- Links or other relevant context.
- The date or approximate time.
- Any additional information that may help the maintainers understand the situation.

Please avoid posting sensitive personal information in public issues or discussions.

Reports will be reviewed in good faith. Retaliation against someone for making a good-faith report or participating in an investigation is unacceptable.

For security vulnerabilities, please follow the instructions in the relevant repository's `SECURITY.md` file instead.

## Responsibilities of Maintainers

Maintainers are expected to:

- Communicate community expectations clearly.
- Apply this Code fairly and consistently.
- Evaluate reports in good faith.
- Protect confidential information where reasonably possible.
- Explain moderation decisions when appropriate.
- Avoid abusing maintainer privileges or using them to target individuals unfairly.

## Attribution and Updates

This Code of Conduct is inspired by established open-source community practices and may be updated as the Novum Labs community grows.

We encourage all contributors to help build a culture of respect, intellectual honesty, responsible innovation, and shared learning.
