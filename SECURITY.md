# Security Policy

## Our Commitment

Novum Labs is committed to building secure, reliable, and responsible software. We take security reports seriously and appreciate the efforts of researchers, developers, and users who help us identify and address vulnerabilities.

This policy provides guidance for reporting potential security issues affecting Novum Labs open-source projects.

## Supported Projects

This policy provides general security-reporting guidance for repositories maintained by Novum Labs.

Individual repositories may specify their own supported versions, security policies, or reporting procedures. Please check the relevant repository for project-specific information.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

**Please do not report suspected security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues, discussions, or pull requests.**

Where available, use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting feature on the affected repository.

If private vulnerability reporting is unavailable, contact the maintainers through an established private contact channel listed in the relevant repository or on the official Novum Labs website.

Please include as much of the following information as you can safely provide:

- **Affected project:** Repository name and affected component.
- **Description:** A clear explanation of the suspected vulnerability.
- **Reproduction steps:** Minimal, safe steps to reproduce the issue.
- **Potential impact:** What an attacker might be able to do.
- **Evidence:** Relevant logs, screenshots, or proof-of-concept material, with secrets and personal information removed.
- **Suggested mitigation:** Any proposed fix or workaround, if available.

Please do not include passwords, API keys, access tokens, private user data, or other sensitive information in your report.

## Our Response Process

When a report is received, maintainers will make reasonable efforts to:

1. Acknowledge the report when practicable.
2. Assess the reported issue and its potential severity.
3. Investigate the issue and determine appropriate mitigation.
4. Develop and validate a fix where necessary.
5. Coordinate remediation and public disclosure with the reporter when appropriate.

Response and remediation times may vary according to the issue's severity, complexity, and the project's maintenance capacity.

## Responsible Disclosure

We request that reporters:

- Allow maintainers a reasonable opportunity to investigate and address reported vulnerabilities before public disclosure.
- Avoid accessing, modifying, or exposing data belonging to others.
- Avoid disrupting services or degrading system availability.
- Test only systems and resources they are authorized to access.
- Follow applicable laws and respect the privacy and security of other users.

Novum Labs appreciates good-faith security research that helps protect our projects and their users.

## Scope and Limitations

This policy does not constitute authorization to perform security testing against systems, infrastructure, or services operated by Novum Labs or third parties.

This policy also does not guarantee a particular response time, a reward, or a specific outcome for every report.

## Acknowledgments

We value the responsible disclosure efforts of security researchers and community members who help improve the safety and reliability of open-source software.

Thank you for helping make Novum Labs projects more secure.
