# Novum Labs

### AI Research | LLM Development | Software Engineering | Open Source

**Research with rigor. Engineer with purpose. Build for impact.**

Novum Labs is a technology and research initiative focused on advancing artificial intelligence, developing reliable software systems, and transforming research ideas into practical solutions.

We combine scientific exploration, software engineering, and continuous experimentation to build useful AI technologies and open-source tools.

## Our Focus Areas

### Artificial Intelligence Research
- Large Language Models (LLMs) and language-model training.
- Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and model evaluation.
- AI reasoning, intelligent systems, and experimental architectures.
- Reproducible experiments and evidence-driven research.

### AI Engineering and Development
- AI-powered applications and intelligent agents.
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and knowledge systems.
- Full-stack software development and API engineering.
- Model evaluation, deployment, and optimization.

### Open-Source Innovation
- Developer tools and reusable software components.
- Research implementations and reproducible experiments.
- Technical documentation, benchmarks, and learning resources.
- Collaboration with researchers and the developer community.

## Our Research Initiatives

**Doubtgate** — An evolving software project within the Novum Labs ecosystem.

**LLM Research** — Building and validating the foundations of language-model training, data processing, and evaluation.

**QuantumGPT** — A planned research initiative exploring language models for fundamental physics, including quantum mechanics, string theory, and quantum gravity.

## Our Engineering Principles

- **Scientific Integrity:** Make claims supported by evidence and experiments.
- **Reproducibility:** Document methods, configurations, datasets, and evaluation procedures.
- **Engineering Excellence:** Build maintainable, tested, and well-documented software.
- **Responsible AI:** Communicate limitations, risks, and appropriate uses.
- **Open Collaboration:** Encourage constructive feedback and meaningful contributions.

## 💼 Investor Relations & Capital Allocation

**Novum Labs Private Limited** is an Indian technology entity incorporated in 2025 under the **Companies Act, 2013**, focusing on DeepTech, Artificial Intelligence, and EdTech research.

### 🏛️ Regulatory Compliance & Investment Framework

We welcome equity investment from domestic and international venture capital funds, institutional investors, and accredited angel networks under Indian regulatory guidelines:

| Investor Category | Permissible Instruments | Applicable Regulatory Framework | Compliance Requirements |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Domestic Investors** (Indian Angels, AIFs, VCs) | Equity Shares, Compulsorily Convertible Preference Shares (CCPS) | Companies Act, 2013 (Sec 42 & Sec 62(1)(c) - Private Placement) | RoC Filings (Form PAS-4, PAS-3, MGT-14) |
| **Foreign Investors** (Overseas VCs, Foreign Angels) | Equity Shares, CCPS (under Automatic Route) | FEMA / RBI Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) Regulations | FC-GPR filing on RBI FIRMS Portal within 30 days of allotment |

*Note: Convertible Notes will be enabled upon completion of our DPIIT Startup Recognition process.*

### 🚀 Standard Equity Investment Channels
* **Compulsorily Convertible Preference Shares (CCPS):** Preferred route for seed and Series A investors to protect equity terms prior to conversion.
* **Equity Shares:** Issued at or above Fair Market Value (FMV) as determined by a CA/Registered Valuer.
