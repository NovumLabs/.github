## Featured Research and Engineering Initiatives

### AEGIS — Reliable AI-Agent Systems

**A Runtime Assurance and Autonomous Recovery Framework for Reliable AI-Agent Systems**

AEGIS is a Novum Labs development initiative exploring how AI-agent systems can be made more dependable through runtime checks, failure detection, validation, recovery strategies, and traceable execution.

Our goal is to investigate engineering approaches that improve the reliability and accountability of autonomous AI systems.

### BAYES — Scientific Experiment and Evidence Ledger

BAYES is an early-stage Python prototype for organizing hypotheses, experiments, and supporting evidence.

Its current capabilities include structured experiment records, evidence tracking, persistent storage, JSON reporting, a command-line interface, and automated tests. The project explores how scientific workflows can become more organized, transparent, and reproducible.

### Doubtgate — Software Development

Doubtgate is an evolving software project within the Novum Labs ecosystem. Visit our repositories for its current implementation and development progress.

### LLM Research

We are exploring the foundations of language-model development, including data preparation, training workflows, evaluation, and experimental model architectures.

### QuantumGPT — Planned Research Initiative

QuantumGPT is a planned research direction exploring language-model approaches to fundamental physics, including quantum mechanics, string theory, and quantum gravity. Its implementation status and research milestones will be communicated as the work progresses.

## Our Engineering Principles

- **Scientific Integrity:** Make claims that are proportionate to the available evidence.
- **Reproducibility:** Document methods, configurations, experiments, and evaluation procedures.
- **Engineering Excellence:** Build maintainable, tested, and well-documented software.
- **Responsible AI:** Communicate system limitations, risks, and appropriate uses.
- **Continuous Evaluation:** Test assumptions, investigate failures, and improve implementations.
- **Open Collaboration:** Welcome constructive feedback and meaningful contributions.
